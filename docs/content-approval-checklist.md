# Pench Big Cat Nature Resort — Content & Naming Story Approval Checklist

This document tracks all proposed naming concepts, room inventories, amenity designations, and wildlife claims from the Telzon Marketing Naming Story Draft 2.

> **Status Definitions**:
> - `approved`: Confirmed by resort ownership for public website display.
> - `pending`: Proposed concept; displayed on website as "concept inspired by Jugni" with owner verification flagged.
> - `rejected`: Omitted from public view until further instruction.

## 3. Wildlife & Conservation Disclaimers
